My name is Owen Moore, a 3D modeller with a passion for cars, character design, and whatever flows through my imagination. I seek to create the best models I can with the occasional 3D print. I have over 4 years’ experience in the space and am always willing to learn.

I use a range of tools to take a model from its first design through to the finished product, whether it ends up as a physical print or as an asset in a game. I also like to switch things up and use Unreal Engine 5 to make small games from time to time.

I create designs in Figma to model from, and I make all my models in Blender. I work with two game engines and am most active on X.
